SpearLine Vis

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · Published by Mason Chavez

SpearLine Vis is a dive-visibility forecast app. It is built so that nothing that identifies you is sent to us. This policy explains exactly what happens to data in the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting services. We (the developer) do not receive any information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

Your dive log (including dive locations you pin on the map, fish caught and seen, and conditions), saved spots, spot tuning, settings and alert preferences are stored only on your device. They are included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you use it), which are governed by Apple's privacy terms, not ours. If you use "Export backup", the app creates a file that you choose where to save or send; we never see it. Deleting the app deletes this data from the device.

Forecast data and third-party services

To build forecasts and draw the map, the app downloads public data directly from these providers, using the fixed coordinates of the dive sites in the app or the map area you are viewing. Your location is never used or sent.

  • Open-Meteo: waves, wind and rain forecasts
  • NASA Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS): satellite chlorophyll images
  • NOAA CO-OPS: tide predictions
  • OpenStreetMap (tile servers run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation): the base map. When you view the map, the app requests the map squares for the area on screen.
  • NOAA Office of Coast Survey: the optional nautical chart layer, requested the same way when you turn it on.

As with any internet request, these providers can see your device's IP address and basic request information, and they handle it under their own privacy policies. The requests contain no personal information from the app.

Notifications

If you turn on clear-water alerts, the app schedules them on your device from the forecast it already has. No notification service or server is involved, and you can turn alerts off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
