SpearLine Vis

Hour-by-hour underwater visibility forecasts for 78 Southern California dive spots, about a week out: Goleta and Gaviota, Ventura and Rincon, Malibu and County Line, Palos Verdes, Catalina and the Channel Islands.

Built from satellite chlorophyll, swell exposure, wind and rain runoff, and tuned by your own dive log. Estimates only; dive at your own risk.
